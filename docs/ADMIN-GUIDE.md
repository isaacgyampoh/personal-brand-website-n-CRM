# Studio — quick guide for the business owner

Everything is done in your browser at **/admin**. You never need to touch code or the server.

## First time
1. Open the one-time link you were sent, choose a password (10+ characters).
2. Add Studio to an authenticator app on your phone and **keep the 10 recovery codes** somewhere safe.
3. Open **Get started** (left menu). Your details, biography, photos, services and speaking engagements are already imported; each step shows exactly what is still missing and turns green by itself when it's done.

## Everyday tasks
| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Update your name, title, biography, expertise, recognition, contact details, social links, logo, photo, colours or business details | **Business profile** — saved instantly to the website |
| Change wording, photos or layout of a page | **Website** → open the page → edit → **Preview** → **Publish** (every section has *Hide this section* if you want it off the site without deleting it) |
| Change logo, colours, fonts, bio | **Website → Design** |
| Upload or crop photos | **Media** (click the important part of a photo to set its focal point; *Create crop* makes a banner or square copy) |
| Add or change a coaching service and price | **Services** (set hours under **Bookings → Availability**) |
| Create a masterclass | **Masterclasses → Quick masterclass** — six steps, then *Publish* and *Copy link* |
| See and manage bookings | **Bookings** (reschedule, cancel, add a meeting link) |
| See registrations and check people in | **Masterclasses** → open one → *Attendees* |
| Look up a client | **Search** box at the top of the menu, or **Contacts** |
| Write an article | **Content → New article** |
| See money in and out, download Excel | **Finance** |
| Ask a happy client for a testimonial | **Contacts** → open the client → *Send testimonial request* (only real words, with their permission, are ever published) |
| Connect Paystack, email, Google, Zoom | **Integrations** — each card has step-by-step instructions and a *Test connection* button |
| Add a team member | **Business settings → Team & roles** |

## Good to know
- **"Connected"** on an integration means it has really been tested. Anything else means it isn't working yet.
- Payments start in **test mode** — no real money moves until we switch to live together.
- Studio may ask for your password again before sensitive changes (payments, team, credentials). That's deliberate.
- Lost your phone? Sign in with a recovery code. Lost those too? Contact your technical administrator to reset two-factor sign-in.
- Privacy Policy and Terms show "being finalised" until you paste your reviewed text into those pages and publish them.
